Italy_Campaign_43_45 (Italian Campaign 1943-1945)

July 3, 1943, 96 turns (to May 1945). Full Italian Theater campaign
cropped from WITW+WITE v0.9.65 for WiTE2 v1.05.02, historically
calibrated against official WITW Battleground Italy 43-45.

Map & Performance Crop:
- Playable area: NW (57,202) - SE (176,318), 13,804 hexes (-89.71%)
  covering North Africa, Malta, Sicily, Sardinia/Corsica, the entire
  Italian peninsula, Po Valley, and Alpine passes; Southern France/
  Spain and Balkans edges cleared.
- Retained forces: 810 units (205 airbases), 337 air groups, and
  435 locations (60 ports).
- Stripped/disabled off-map: 9,676 units, 1,842 air groups, and
  3,628 out-of-bounds city economies for fast turn resolution.

Historical Calibration (WITW Battleground Italy 43-45):
- Preserves Sep 8, 1943 Italy Surrenders and Italy events; disables
  Berlin Captured and Eastern/Nordic Theater events.
- Restores 36 WITW-verified German reinforcements & elite support
  battalions from France/Austria/Theater Boxes into Army Group C:
  5 HQs (incl. 10th/14th Army & LXXVI Pz Corps), 21 divisions/brigades
  (incl. 1st/2nd/4th FJ & 26th Pz), and 10 Tiger/Ferdinand/Nashorn/
  StuG support battalions.
- Early Sicily Axis/Italian morale calibrated to WITW historical values.

T1 Allied Ops Order:
1. Issue INVADE orders for Husky Amphibious HQs and F9 -> Airborne
   drops first, then prepare mainland landings around Sep 1943.
