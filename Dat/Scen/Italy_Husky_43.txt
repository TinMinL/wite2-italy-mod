Italy_Husky_43 (Operation Husky - July 1943)

July 3, 1943, 8 turns. Standalone Sicily amphibious & airborne
scenario cropped from WITW+WITE v0.9.60 for WiTE2 v1.05.02.

Playable map area is cropped to NW (57,275) - SE (166,314),
reducing the full European grid by 96.83% to cover North African
staging ports (Tunis/Bizerte/Algeria), Malta, Sicily, and southern
Calabria (Reggio). Southern Sardinia edge is cleared.

All 10,038 out-of-theater units, 2,000 air groups, and 3,934
out-of-bounds city economies are disabled/stripped for fast turn
processing. Retained on-theater forces: 424 units (including 55
airbases), 159 air groups, and 119 locations (24 ports).
The Sep 8, 1943 Italy Surrenders and Berlin events are disabled.

T1 Allied Ops Order:
1. Select each Amphibious HQ (TF 81/85/86, TF 545 A/B/C) and issue
   INVADE orders first.
2. Press F9 -> Airborne to launch the 7 Tunisia airborne/glider
   detachments onto adjacent drop hexes before ending the phase.

Both sides Human recommended for initial testing.
Original WITW+WITE by yyuegu; v0.9.60 continuation by Minatoaquadebu.
Italy-only cropped scenario built with wite2-italy-mod.
