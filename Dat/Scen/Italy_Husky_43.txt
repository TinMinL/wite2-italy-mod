Italy_Husky_43 (Operation Husky - July 1943)

July 3, 1943, 8 turns. Standalone Sicily amphibious & airborne
scenario cropped from WITW+WITE v0.9.65 for WiTE2 v1.05.02,
historically calibrated against official WITW Operation Husky.

Map & Performance Crop:
- Playable area: NW (57,270) - SE (166,314), 4,796 hexes (-96.43%)
  covering North African staging ports (Algeria/Tunis/Bizerte),
  Malta, Sicily, and southern Calabria (Reggio); Sardinia cleared.
- Retained forces: 435 units (61 airbases), 185 air groups, and
  129 locations (25 ports).
- Stripped/disabled off-map: 10,051 units, 1,994 air groups, and
  3,925 out-of-bounds city economies for fast turn resolution.

Historical Calibration (WITW Operation Husky):
- 29th PzGr Division staged in southern Calabria; airlifted 1st
  Fallschirmjaeger-Division restored to arrive at Catania (Delay 1).
- Axis/Italian morale calibrated to WITW; 11 non-Husky Salerno and
  peninsula Allied divisions frozen for 12 turns.
- Sep 8, 1943 Italy Surrenders and Berlin Captured events disabled.

T1 Allied Ops Order:
1. Select each Amphibious HQ (TF 81/85/86, TF 545 A/B/C) and issue
   INVADE orders first.
2. Press F9 -> Airborne to drop the 7 Tunisia airborne/glider
   detachments onto adjacent landing zones before ending the phase.

Both sides Human recommended for initial testing.
